HOW IT WORKS

Automated 3-Step Parking Workflow

1️⃣

Vehicle Arrival & Intake

Staff registers vehicle number and owner details. System matches vehicle type (Car/Bike) and assigns best available bay.

2️⃣

Real-Time Tracking

Bay status is locked as 'Occupied'. Live duration counter tracks active parking time across multi-level floors.

3️⃣

Checkout & Billing

Exit is logged, parking fee is calculated based on exact hours, slot is restored to 'Available', and a receipt is printed.
